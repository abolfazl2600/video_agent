# Art direction — Navy & Yellow Isometric Miniatures

## North star
A calm near-black navy-teal field. One tiny, matte **warm-yellow aircraft** is the hero, seen almost orthographically from above. A few thin pale-blue flight-trail strokes. No busy sky or complex city backdrop. Preserve large regions of negative space.

## Design tokens
- Navy base: `#062E3A` and `#073440`; teal gradients must stay subtle.
- Airplane accent: `#F4BE39` / soft yellow, with cream highlights.
- Secondary: pale blue `#80BED2`, warm cream `#F1F0E8` and muted parcel tan `#D4AB78`.
- Props: simplified toy clay with gentle corner bevels, 30–45° isometric / orthographic composition, low contrast shadows, matte surfaces.
- The central character/parcel or aircraft should occupy a small part of the composition, leaving significant space for text (the reference airplane is especially small).
- Avoid excessive photorealism, detailed city architecture, dense UI, strong lens effects, overly saturated cyan, or busy particle effects.

## Visual continuity
The same sender girl, traveler, compact package, suitcase and yellow plane recur consistently across the five scenes. All five illustrations are *conceptual key frames* rather than final continuous shots: reconcile clothing, scale, airplane design and backgrounds when building models.

## Video coding notes
1. Model assets separately (sender, traveler, parcel, suitcase, plane, phone, two floating endpoints).
2. Use a real orthographic camera and persistent lighting/camera to preserve continuity.
3. Keep 5 scenes at 0–3 / 3–7 / 7–11 / 11–16 / 16–20 seconds.
4. Compose Persian UI and voiceover as separate layers; use the repo's shared Vazirmatn fonts.
5. For the 7–11s scene, depict Advertio as an illustrated mockup, not a false screenshot.
6. In 11–16s, animate the **blue connection line** and a passenger message card.
7. In 16–20s, animate a small yellow plane and central phone, then clear CTA.
8. Render **Preview** first. Final 30 fps only on explicit request.
