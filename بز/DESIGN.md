# Visual identity
Modern architectural line illustration derived from the user's airport image. A continuous transition from the supplied rear-quarter photo enters a real Three.js scene, then camera orbits behind the aircraft before its takeoff. Cargo stays visible and secured to the rear fuselage.
## Palette
Sky #eaf2f3, body #e7f0f1, contour #204c5c, focus #08746f, focus panel #d2ebe9, accent #20ab9e, luggage #efae68.
## Typography
Noto Kufi Arabic Bold for the focal phrase. Noto Sans Arabic Regular for the surrounding sentence. RTL shaping, generous safe margins.
## Motion
Smooth orbit, accelerating runway translation, positive nose-up rotation and climbing path, retracting landing gear. The focus phrase reveals RTL and has a separate moving underline. Preserve a long readable final hold.
## Avoid
No triangular wireframe clutter, neon treatment, random camera shake, unrelated stock planes, English UI labels, or substitutions for the Persian wording.
